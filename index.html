THE STORY

A voice inherited, a voice made my own.

A senior disciple of Padma Shri Mandolin U. Srinivas — shaped by nearly nine years in the gurukul, carrying his guru's bani onto stages across India and the world.

01

Nine years in the gurukul

A senior disciple of Padma Shri Mandolin U. Srinivas, Vishnu underwent nearly nine years of rigorous, immersive training with his guru.

02

Preserving the bani

His musicianship is distinguished by command over raga alapana, kalpana swaras, niraval and manodharma — supported by laya, tonal clarity and gamaka articulation.

03

On the classical stage

Balancing technical precision with aesthetic restraint, his concerts bring an analytical depth to the classical Carnatic tradition.
